# Inspiration sites

Real sites named across three reels, with what was actually visible on screen. Study these
instead of inventing a look — every one of them is a single committed idea executed fully.

⚠️ These are third-party sites captured Sept 2026; any of them can go down or be redesigned.
Check before citing one to a client.

## How to use these

1. **Pick one** whose *mechanism* matches the job, not whose colours you like.
2. Name the mechanism out loud before building — "oversized cropped wordmark over product
   photography", "one 3D object in empty white", "borderless thumbnail wall".
3. Cross-check against a separate measured study of award sites: the finding is that
   award-winning sites usually carry the whole page on **one** effect (lircle.co is entirely
   words flipping grey→solid as the scroll line passes; indisea.com uses zero animation
   libraries, just three sticky cards). Ambition should go into *commitment*, not *count*.
