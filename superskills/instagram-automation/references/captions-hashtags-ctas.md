# Captions, hashtags and CTAs

> Distilled from: viral-instagram-reels (vyralcontent/content-skills, MIT), caption-writer-sms (blacktwist/social-media-skills, MIT), instagram-marketing / ig-caption-writer, ig-hashtag-strategist, ig-humanizer (sergebulaev/instagram-skills, MIT).

> ToS: official Graph API / Instagram API only (Business or Creator), user-approved actions, no bots or cold DMs, scraping is HIGH risk and never the default.

A caption has three jobs, in this order: hook (before the fold), payoff, then one CTA. On Instagram the caption and the on-screen text are also what search reads. Instagram search indexes them, and public posts can show up in Google results.

## Length and limits

| Item | Rule |
|---|---|
| Hard cap | 2,200 characters |
| The fold | About **125 characters** show before "more". The hook has to stand alone in them. |
| Reel caption | 100-300 characters. The video is the hook, and the caption adds the keyword and the CTA. |
| Single image | 80-300 characters, or longer if it tells a real story |
| Carousel | 150-800 characters. The slides carry the payload, and the caption restates the hook and asks. |
| Hashtags | 30 is the platform maximum. **Use 3-5.** |
| Links | Not clickable in captions. Use the bio link, a Story link sticker, or a DM delivery (`dms-and-comments.md`). |

## Structure

```
[Line 1: keyword phrase or hook, sentence case, <=125 chars, makes sense alone]

[1-3 short lines: expand the hook / payoff, plain words, white space between beats]

[One CTA naming the reason or the person]

[3-5 hashtags]
```

Example (Reel):

```
Niacinamide for oily skin: the layering order most people get wrong.

Put it on before the moisturiser, not after, or the breakouts come back faster than the serum works.

Send this to the friend who buys every new serum.

#oilyskincare #niacinamide #skincareroutine #skincare
```

## Hook line (first 125 characters)

- **State the payoff, the tension or the number up front.** "I cut editing from 6 hours to 47 minutes" beats "Some thoughts on editing".
- **Lead with the exact words a stranger would search for.** Save the clever lines for later in the caption.
- **Line 1 must make sense without line 2.** The fold hides everything after it.
- **Use odd, exact numbers with a named referent** ("47 minutes", "$873 in March"). They read as measured. Never invent them.
- **Hook shapes that work:**
  - number-first result
  - contrarian truth you would defend in person
  - relatable cold open ("opening Canva for one graphic and closing it 90 minutes later")
  - short confession with a real cost

## CTA: one, specific, platform-native

| Goal | CTA |
|---|---|
| Reach (sends) | "Send this to [specific person] who [specific situation]." |
| Saves | "Save this for [the moment they'll need it]." |
| Comments | A real, specific question ("Which of the 3 would you cut first?") |
| Follows | "Follow for [specific recurring value], every [day]." |
| Leads | "Comment GUIDE and I'll DM it to you." Only if a compliant comment-to-DM automation and the deliverable both exist (`dms-and-comments.md`). |

Never use:

- engagement bait ("double tap if", "comment YES", "tag 3 friends"), which is downranked
- dead closers ("thoughts?", "what do you think?")
- three competing asks
- "link in bio" when there is no link

## Hashtags: size, not volume

Hashtags label the topic. They are not a reach engine. **3-5 relevant tags, at the end of the caption** (the first comment also works).

| Tier | Approx. posts | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Niche | under 50k | 2-3. A smaller account can actually rank here for hours. |
| Mid | 50k-500k | 1-2 |
| Broad | 500k+ | 0-1, as a category label only |

- Every tag must describe the actual post. Off-topic popular tags send the post to the wrong audience.
- Rotate tag sets by topic. Identical tag blocks on every post look automated.
- Skip engagement tags (#follow4follow, #like4like) and anything flagged.
- Check tag sizes in the app's search. The publishing API doesn't expose post counts, and hashtag search via the API is limited to 30 unique tags per 7 days.
- Branded or campaign tags can take one niche slot.

## On-screen text and alt text

- Use the same keyword phrase on the first frame or slide 1 **and** in caption line 1. Matching vocabulary strengthens the topic signal.
- Write alt text for every image: what is shown plus the key text on the slide. It helps accessibility and helps Instagram understand the post.

## Voice and AI-tell scrub

Write in the user's register. Read 3+ of their past captions and match their emoji habits, casing, punctuation and sentence length. Then scrub:

- **Vocabulary clusters:** 3 or more of these in one paragraph means rewrite the paragraph.
  - leverage, streamline, robust, seamless, delve, harness, foster, elevate, empower, unlock
  - crucial, notably, comprehensive, insights, landscape, journey, fundamentally, ultimately
  - One of these words alone is fine.
- **Single-hit rewrites:**
  - "It's not X, it's Y"
  - "Here's the thing", "The result?", "Let's talk about"
  - "In today's fast-paced world"
  - "game-changer", "level up", "deep dive"
  - "real talk", "not gonna lie" and other sincerity announcements
  - "POV:" on something that isn't a POV
- **Em dashes:** at most about 1 per 100 words. Replace extras with a comma, colon, parentheses or line break, never with a full stop (that creates fragment stacks).
- **No staccato stacks** ("No fluff. No filler. Just results."). Allow at most one natural rule-of-three, with concrete items.
- **Emoji:** 0-3, placed on purpose, never sprinkled.
- **Capitalisation:** names always capitalised. Lowercase sentence starts are fine if that's the user's voice.
- **Keep** the user's own quirks. Don't scrub their voice into a generic one.

## Variants

When asked, give 2-3 variants that differ by **hook formula** or **CTA goal**, not by synonyms. Label each one with its primary goal (sends, saves, comments or follows).

## Checklist

- [ ] Line 1 stands alone within 125 characters and carries the keyword or number.
- [ ] One idea and one CTA, no engagement bait, no dead closer.
- [ ] 3-5 relevant, size-mixed hashtags at the end.
- [ ] Caption and on-screen text use the same keyword phrase. Alt text is drafted.
- [ ] Voice matches the samples, the AI-tell scrub is done, and there are no invented numbers.
- [ ] A media note is included (Instagram has no text-only posts): which image or video goes with this caption.
