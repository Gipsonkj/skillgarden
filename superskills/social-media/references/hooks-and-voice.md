# Hooks, voice and shareability

Use this for any single post, caption, first line, or "make this sound like me / less like AI".

## 1. Voice comes before copy

- Read the user's voice profile if one exists. If not, build one from 10-25 of their **own** recent posts (or newsletter, emails), then confirm it with them before writing at volume. Never guess a personality.
- Capture: sentence length, casing habits, punctuation, words they use, words they never use, emoji habits, how they open and close, topics they avoid.
- Re-anchor on the profile before **each** post in a batch. Tone drifts toward generic marketing copy by post 10-15 of a 20-post batch.

## 2. The first line does the work

Readers decide in the first line (text) or first 1-3 seconds (video). Write the body first, then write 5-7 hook options across different families, then pick.

| Family | Shape | Best for |
|---|---|---|
| Specific result | "[Result with an odd, real number] in [time]." | Saves, follows |
| Data point | "[Unrounded number] [what it measures]." + one line on why it matters | Bookmarks, shares |
| Story opener | "Last week, [unexpected specific thing] happened." / "I almost [mistake]." | Dwell, comments |
| Before/after | "[Time] ago I [state]. Today [state]." | Dwell, follows |
| Value promise | "How to [outcome] without [common pain]:" / "[N] [things] that [outcome]:" | Saves |
| Contrarian | "[Common advice] cost us [specific result]." / "I stopped [practice] and [result]." | Replies, reposts |
| Confession / build in public | "[Project] did [real metric, good or bad]. The part nobody posts: [cost]." | Replies |
| Breakdown | "[Company/person] just [did thing]. Here is what happened, with numbers." | Reposts |

Rules for every hook:
- **Specific beats vague:** "$47,329" not "good revenue"; "47 days" not "a while"; name the tool, company or place.
- **Name the topic in the first 10 words** so the platform can route it to interested people.
- **True and defensible.** Never invent a number. A contrarian claim you would not defend in the replies will get ratioed.
- One idea per post. Two ideas means two posts or a thread.
- On X there is no "see more": the first line is the whole post for most readers. On LinkedIn the hook must land before ~210 characters, on Instagram before ~125.

## 3. Post structures that hold up

- **Story:** hook -> scene -> problem -> what you tried -> turning point -> result -> the lesson for the reader.
- **Contrarian:** claim -> what changed your mind (with a number) -> 2-3 reasons -> what to do instead.
- **List:** "[N] things I learned about [topic] after [credential]" -> numbered points with one line each -> wrap-up insight.
- **How-to:** outcome promise -> 3-5 steps, each with why it matters -> expected result.
- **Caption (feed/short video):** hook -> one concrete benefit -> one CTA -> 3-5 relevant hashtags (Instagram/TikTok only).

## 4. No AI tells (scrub before delivering)

Readers scroll past copy that reads as generated, and on LinkedIn they say so in the comments. Remove:

| Tell | Fix |
|---|---|
| Contrast reveals: "It's not X, it's Y." "Not because X. Because Y." | State Y directly with the reason |
| Negation lists: "No X, no Y, no Z." | Say what does happen (one plain "No card required" is fine) |
| Self-answered questions and colon reveals: "The result? 3x faster." | "It got 3x faster." |
| Stock phrases: "unlock", "game-changer", "in today's fast-paced world", "take it to the next level", "excited to share", "here's the thing" | Plain words |
| AI vocabulary density: leverage, delve, robust, seamless, streamline, foster, landscape, crucial, notably | One such word is English; three in one post means rewrite |
| Performed sincerity: "I'll be honest...", "Real talk", "Unpopular opinion:" on a popular take | State the fact flat |
| Broetry: one short sentence per line for the whole post | Write paragraphs; break lines where a person would pause |
| Engagement bait: "Agree?", "Thoughts?", "Read that again", "Comment YES" | End on the point, or a specific question you actually want answered |
| Emoji as bullets on every line | 0-1 emoji unless the user's voice uses more |
| Dash-heavy rhythm | At most one em dash per post; prefer a period, comma or line break |
| Hollow rule of three: "faster, cheaper, better" | One concrete triple at most per post |

Also delete any CTA, moral, or "what do you think?" that the source did not earn.

## 5. CTAs

- One CTA per post. Match it to the goal: save, share, follow, reply with a specific answer, click (link in comment/bio where links hurt reach), book.
- "Comment [keyword] and I'll send it" works for lead magnets but must be honoured manually or by an approved tool, and repeated keyword bait can be read as engagement bait on X.
- Do not ask for a like.

## 6. Shareability check (STEPPS)

Jonah Berger's six drivers of word of mouth. Score each 0 (absent), 1 (weak), 2 (strong). A post or campaign scoring under 6/12 relies on luck; aim for at least two drivers at 2.

| Driver | Question | Cheap fix |
|---|---|---|
| Social currency | Does sharing it make the sharer look smart or in-the-know? | Add an insider fact, an exact benchmark, or a result people want to cite |
| Triggers | Will something in daily life remind people of it? | Tie it to a frequent moment (Monday standup, payday, coffee) rather than a rare one |
| Emotion | Does it create high-arousal feeling (awe, amusement, excitement, justified anger)? | Reframe the same facts for surprise; avoid low-arousal "nice" |
| Public | Can others see people using or sharing it? | Shareable output, visible badge, "made with" credit |
| Practical value | Would someone forward it to help a friend? | Package as a checklist, numbered steps, or a tool; narrow audience = more forwarding |
| Stories | Is the brand essential to a retellable story? | If the story can be retold without the brand, rework it |

Ethics line: no false scarcity, no manufactured outrage, no invented testimonials or origin stories.

## 7. Quick self-check

- [ ] First line is specific, true, and names the topic
- [ ] One idea; one CTA
- [ ] Sounds like the user (checked against the voice profile)
- [ ] No item from the AI-tells table
- [ ] Fits the platform's limit and truncation point (platform-playbook.md)
- [ ] Would someone send this to a friend, or save it? If not, sharpen it
