# Analytics, audits, community and growth

Use this for "why is engagement down", "audit our social", "grow my following", weekly reports, community management rules, and engagement routines.

## 1. Metrics that matter

| Layer | Metrics | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Awareness | Reach, impressions, follower growth **rate** | Raw follower count and impressions alone are vanity |
| Engagement | Engagement rate, comments, shares/reposts, saves/bookmarks, DM shares | Comments, shares and saves outweigh likes on every platform |
| Video | Average watch %, completion rate, follows per 1,000 views | Completion tells you if the hook paid off |
| Conversion | Link clicks, profile visits, DMs started, leads/sales attributed (UTMs, codes) | Tie at least one metric to the business goal |

**Engagement rate** = (likes + comments + shares + saves) / reach x 100 (use impressions if reach is not available, and say which).

Rough healthy targets (vary by niche and size; compare against the account's own 90-day median first):

| Platform | Engagement rate | Follower growth |
|---|---|---|
| LinkedIn | > 3% | > 5% / month for a growing account |
| X | > 1% | same |
| Instagram | > 2% (1-3% typical at scale, higher for small accounts) | same |
| Click-through on link posts | > 1% | |

## 2. Weekly review (30 minutes)

1. Top 3 posts: why did they work? (hook, format, topic, time)
2. Bottom 3 posts: what failed? (usually the hook or a mismatch of format and content)
3. Follower growth and engagement-rate trend vs last 4 weeks
4. Best posting times from the account's own data
5. One change to test next week, with the metric that will judge it

If the user's scheduler or API exposes post stats, pull them at the start of each session and feed them into this review instead of guessing (publishing-apis.md).

## 3. Diagnose and fix

| Symptom | Likely cause | Actions |
|---|---|---|
| Engagement rate below the account's median | Hooks not landing, wrong format | Audit the last 20 posts; test new hooks on the same topics; try another format |
| Reach declining | Links in body, low cadence, no early engagement | Move links to comments/replies; restore cadence; be present the first 30-60 min; test video |
| High reach, few follows | Content attracts the wrong people or no reason to follow | Tighten niche; add a follow reason in the CTA and bio; check the pinned post |
| Saves high, shares low | Useful but not social | Add a story, opinion or social-currency angle (hooks-and-voice.md STEPPS) |
| Comments negative | Tone or claim problem | Check facts; adjust tone; respond per the rules below |
| Posting dropped below 3/week | Capacity | Batch-create; cut to a cadence the user can hold |
| 100% promotional feed | Mix | Move to <= 10-20% promotion; add useful and proof posts |
| Same format every post | Format fatigue | Rotate text, carousel, video, poll |

## 4. Social audit checklist

**Profile (each platform)**
- [ ] Recognizable photo, consistent across platforms
- [ ] Bio states a clear value proposition, not a job-title list; no hashtags on X
- [ ] Link goes to a relevant landing page with UTMs, not just the homepage
- [ ] Pinned/featured post is recent and the strongest piece

**Content (last 30 posts)**
- [ ] Regular cadence, not bursts and gaps
- [ ] Balanced pillar mix (strategy-calendar.md)
- [ ] Format variety
- [ ] Voice consistent with the brand
- [ ] No AI tells (hooks-and-voice.md)

**Engagement**
- [ ] Replies to comments within 2 business hours
- [ ] Replies are real, not "thanks!"
- [ ] Outbound engagement on others' posts every day
- [ ] Present in relevant groups, communities, threads

Deliver: findings per section, rated, then a prioritized fix list (impact x effort), then a 30-day plan.

## 5. Daily engagement routine (30 minutes)

1. Reply to every comment on your posts (5 min).
2. Comment on 5-10 posts from target accounts, using listening-research.md tiers (15 min).
3. Repost or quote with added insight (5 min).
4. Send 2-3 genuine DMs to new connections or engaged followers (5 min).

Rule of thumb: spend as much time engaging with others as publishing. Relationship building: list 20-50 accounts in the space, engage consistently, share their work with credit, then collaborate (podcasts, co-created posts).

## 6. Community management rules

| Situation | Response |
|---|---|
| Product question | Answer within 2 business hours |
| Complaint | Acknowledge publicly, resolve privately (DM/email), follow up publicly when fixed |
| Praise | Thank, then amplify with a reshare or quote (with permission for UGC) |
| Troll | Ignore unless it states something factually wrong; correct the fact once, calmly |
| Crisis or legal/safety issue | Pause scheduled posts, escalate to the owner, no improvised statements |
| Industry discussion | Add real value; no self-promotion |

Write these as a short escalation sheet for any team that shares the accounts.

## 7. Growth levers (organic)

1. **Consistency** on a sustainable cadence.
2. **Replies and comments** on bigger accounts in the niche (often the fastest path for small accounts).
3. **Collaboration** with complementary accounts.
4. **Repurposing** one long piece into 5-10 posts.
5. **Trends** only when relevant and fast.
6. **Recurring series** people can anticipate.
7. **Owned community** (newsletter, Discord, group) so reach does not depend on one algorithm.
8. **Creators and ambassadors** for borrowed trust (influencer-marketing.md).

Growth plans for X specifically: x-threads.md sections 7-8.

## 8. What to test (one variable at a time)

Hooks (same content, new first line), length, format, posting time, CTA, thumbnail or slide 1. Run each test on at least 3-5 posts before concluding; single posts are noisy.
