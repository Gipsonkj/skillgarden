# Short-form video: TikTok, Reels, Shorts

Use this for video scripts, hooks, reel ideas, captions/subtitles, and video posting plans. For rendering video in code (Remotion, HyperFrames) or with video models, use a motion-graphics or video skill; this file covers what to say and show.

## 1. Specs

| Platform | Start length | Ratio | Character |
|---|---|---|---|
| TikTok | 15-60 s (start under 30 s) | 9:16 | Raw, native, trend-aware; search-indexed captions |
| Instagram Reels | 15-30 s | 9:16 | A bit more polished; saves and shares drive reach |
| YouTube Shorts | 30-60 s | 9:16 | Searchable titles; feeds the long-form channel |

Safe area: keep text and faces out of the top ~10% and bottom ~20% of the frame, where the app's buttons and captions sit.

## 2. The first 3 seconds

Stack three hooks in the first second: **visual** (movement, a result, a face reacting), **spoken**, and **on-screen text** (most people watch muted). Do not build up to the point.

Hook families (fill with the real specifics):
- Result first: show the finished outcome, then "here is how".
- Problem call-out: "If your [thing] keeps [problem], watch this."
- Mistake: "Stop [common practice]. It cost me [specific result]."
- Experiment: "I tried [thing] for 30 days. Here is what changed."
- Story: "Three months ago I [state]. Today [state]."
- Contrarian: "[Common advice] is wrong for [audience], and here is the test."

## 3. Structures with timings

```
PROBLEM-SOLUTION (15-30 s)
0-3   Hook: name the problem
3-10  Why it matters (agitate, one example)
10-25 The fix: method, product or tip, shown on screen
25-30 CTA
```
```
LIST (30-60 s)
0-3   "[N] things that [outcome]"
3-50  One item every 5-8 s, each with a visual change
50-60 CTA
```
```
TUTORIAL (30-60 s)
0-3   Show the end result
3-8   "Here's how" + step count
8-50  Steps, one cut per step
50-60 Result again + CTA
```
```
STORY ARC (45-60 s)
0-3   Tease the outcome
3-15  Setup and stakes
15-45 What happened
45-55 Result
55-60 Lesson / CTA
```
```
POV / SKIT (15-30 s)
0-3   On-screen text sets the scene
3-25  Act out the relatable moment
25-30 Twist or punchline
```

## 5. Formats and when to use them

| Format | Use for | Craft notes |
|---|---|---|
| Talking head ("FaceTime with a friend") | Conversion, trust, explaining | Window or ring light, eye contact, open with a specific problem, product enters where it naturally fits |
| Slideshow / photo carousel | Cheap volume, lists, before/after | 2-4 s per slide, one point per slide, first slide creates curiosity |
| Screen recording / demo | Software, workflows | Zoom into the action, highlight the cursor, show the result first; face in corner optional |
| Wall of text over B-roll | Reach and warm-up | Goes far, converts least |
| Hook-and-demo | Products | Aging format; needs a fresh twist |
| B-roll + voiceover | Faceless channels, stories | Cuts every 1-3 s, matched to the voiceover beats; mix wide, medium, close |

Test the same idea in two formats to learn whether the idea or its presentation failed.

## 6. Captions and audio

- **Burned-in subtitles always.** Max 2 lines on screen, 3-5 words per line, bold sans-serif with outline, highlight key words, timed exactly to speech.
- **Trending sound:** only when it fits the message and is still rising; check the platform's commercial music library for business accounts (many trending tracks are not licensed for brands).
- **Original audio:** for teaching, stories, demos, building a recognizable voice.
- **Voiceover:** slightly faster than conversation, varied tone, pauses on key points, quiet room plus noise removal. Duck music under speech.
- Bad audio kills retention faster than bad video.

## 7. Cadence and batching

| Goal | Minimum | Strong |
|---|---|---|
| Growing | 1/day | 2-4/day |
| Maintaining | 3/week | 1/day |
| Testing a new account | 2/week | 5/week |

Batch: ideate 10-20 concepts (30 min) -> script 5-10 (1 h) -> film all talking heads in one session (2 h) -> edit and caption (2-3 h) -> schedule (30 min).

## 8. Reading results and iterating

| Signal | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Completion rate / average watch % | Did hook and body deliver? |
| Saves | Worth returning to |
| Shares | Worth spreading |
| Comments, especially product questions | Real intent; turn repeated questions into reply videos |
| Follows per view | Content-audience fit |

Pivot rules:
- 5+ videos under ~1% completion: change the hooks before anything else.
- High views, few follows: weak CTA or the content attracts the wrong audience.
- High saves, low shares: useful but not social; add a story or a stronger opinion.
- When one works, remake it at once, changing **one variable at a time**: new hook x same format, same hook x another format, same idea x another creator.
- Review daily, judge over 4 weeks. You are looking for a format that wins more than once.

## 9. Common mistakes

Slow hooks; no on-screen text; poor audio; too long (if it can be shorter, cut it); no CTA; ignoring comments in the first hour; reposting horizontal video; the product appearing so early it feels like an ad.
