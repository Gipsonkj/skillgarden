# Platform playbook: limits, formats, what works

Platform limits change. Treat the numbers below as of late 2026 and check the platform's help pages before quoting a limit to a client. For LinkedIn and Instagram depth, also see the `linkedin-automation` and `instagram-automation` super skills.

## Limits at a glance

| Platform | Max text | Visible before "more" | Hashtags (max / use) | Main visual sizes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| X | 280 chars standard; 25,000 Premium | Whole post (280) | none / 0-1 | 4 images per post; 16:9 or 1:1 |
| LinkedIn | 3,000 chars | ~210 chars | no limit / 0-3 at the end | Document PDF 1:1 or 4:5; image 1200x627 |
| Instagram | 2,200 chars caption | ~125 chars | 5 / 3-5 | Feed 1080x1350 (4:5); Reels/Stories 1080x1920 |
| TikTok | 4,000 chars caption | ~150 chars | 5 / 3-5 | 1080x1920 (9:16) |
| Facebook | 63,206 chars | ~1-3 lines | no limit / 1-2 | Link/feed 1200x630 |
| Threads | 500 chars (plus up to 10,000 in a text attachment) | 1-2 lines | 1 topic tag | 4:5 or 1:1 |
| Bluesky | 300 chars | Whole post | none needed | 1:1 or 16:9 |
| YouTube | Title 100 (aim < 60); description 5,000 bytes | Title ~70; description ~100-160 | 15 max (more = all ignored) / 3-5 | Thumbnail 1280x720; Shorts 9:16 |
| Pinterest | Title 100; description 500 | ~50 chars | 20 / 2-5 (keywords matter more) | 2:3 (1000x1500) |

Counting gotchas:
- **X counts each emoji as 2 characters and every URL as 23**, whatever its length. A post that looks like 278 characters can be over.
- YouTube descriptions are limited in **bytes**, so non-Latin text uses up the limit faster. Tags total max 500 characters.
- Hashtags count against every character limit.
- Front-load the message before the truncation point on every platform.

## X

- **Best for:** tech, media, builders, real-time commentary.
- **Works:** a specific claim in the first line, threads that teach, quote posts that add a layer, build-in-public numbers, fast replies.
- **Fails:** pure self-promotion, threads with a weak first post, scheduling everything and never replying.
- **Format:** one idea per post; line breaks as pacing; 0-1 emoji; 0-1 hashtag at the end; link in a reply or later thread post, not post 1 (widely reported to cut reach; test it on your account).
- Full ranking rules and thread craft: x-threads.md.

## LinkedIn

- **Best for:** B2B, decision-makers, recruiting.
- **Works:** personal stories with a business lesson, contrarian takes backed by numbers, behind-the-scenes, original data, document carousels.
- **Fails:** corporate tone, generic motivation, overt promotion, links in the post body.
- **Format:** hook in the first ~210 characters (before "see more"); 1,200-1,500 characters tends to perform; short paragraphs; link in the first comment; tag people only when they are genuinely involved.
- **Algorithm:** the first hour of engagement matters most; comments outweigh reactions; dwell time counts; polls get engagement but do not build authority.
- **Times to test:** Tue-Thu, 7-8am, noon, 5-6pm local.

## Instagram

- **Best for:** visual B2C, lifestyle, ecommerce, 18-44.
- **Works:** Reels (prioritized over static), save-worthy carousels, interactive Stories (polls, questions), UGC with permission.
- **Fails:** low-quality images, text-heavy images, only promotion, ignoring Stories and Reels.
- **Format:** first frame of a Reel must hook; carousel slide 1 is the thumbnail; caption carries the searchable keywords; max 5 hashtags.
- **Signals:** saves and shares beat likes.
- **Times to test:** 11am-1pm, 7-9pm.

## TikTok

- **Best for:** reach, 16-34, entertainment-wrapped education.
- **Works:** native, unpolished video; trending sounds that fit; POV and day-in-the-life; reply-to-comment videos; duets and stitches.
- **Fails:** over-produced ads, hard selling, recycled horizontal video, ignoring trends.
- **Format:** hook in 1-2 seconds; start under 30 seconds; vertical 9:16 only; caption includes the search keyword (captions are indexed); max 5 hashtags.
- **Times to test:** 7-9am, 12-3pm, 7-11pm.

## YouTube

- **Best for:** search-driven tutorials and reviews, long-form trust; also surfaces in Google results and AI answers.
- **Shorts:** 9:16, titles written as searchable phrases, 1-5 hashtags.
- Full SEO and thumbnail guidance: youtube-seo-thumbnails.md.

## Facebook

- **Best for:** local businesses, groups, events, 35+.
- **Works:** groups, native video, live video, local news and events, real discussion questions.
- **Fails:** external links in the post (reach drop), pure promotion, unadapted cross-posts.
- **Format:** 40-80 characters gets the most engagement for simple posts; longer is fine for stories; 1-2 hashtags at most.
- **Times to test:** 1-4pm weekdays.

## Threads and Bluesky

- Conversational and direct; write like a person, not a brand.
- Threads allows one topic tag; Bluesky does not need hashtags. Neither rewards feed-gaming language.
- Do not paste the LinkedIn version and shorten it.

## Pinterest

- A search engine. Lead titles with the keyword, write the description as natural sentences with keywords, link every pin to its article or product. Hashtags are deprioritized.

## Multi-part series on non-thread platforms

| Platform | How to run a series |
|---|---|
| LinkedIn, Facebook | Separate posts labelled "Part 2 of 5"; link or point to the next part; pin part 1 |
| Instagram | Reel series labelled "Part X" on screen and in caption line 1; pin Part 1; or one carousel as the whole thread |
| TikTok | "Part X" on screen, in caption and in voiceover; consistent series hashtag; reply-to-comment videos as later parts; pin Part 1 |
| YouTube | Playlist for long-form, title "[Series] - Part X: [topic]", end screen to the next part; Shorts labelled like TikTok |
| Pinterest | One multi-page pin, or a board with parts titled by keyword first |

Every part must stand alone: viewers on short-video feeds rarely saw Part 1.

## Posting times

The published "best times" are starting points only. After 4-6 weeks, use the account's own analytics (when followers are online, and when the top 10% of posts went out).
