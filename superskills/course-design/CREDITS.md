## Not copied

- **Scripts:** the k12 render scripts (`render_all.sh` pip-installs python-docx at run time; documents are built through docs-office instead); idstack's `bin/` helpers (part of a pipeline that runs an hourly GitHub update check and optional Consensus API lookups; no update checks or third-party calls were carried over); assessment-architect's `export_lms.py` (not in the downloaded copy). The export formats are described in the guides instead.
- **Templates:** assessment-architect's blueprint, project-brief and grade-report templates refer to that repo's course passport and quality gates; their structure is described in assessments-and-feedback.md rather than copied.
- **Dropped claims:** specific forgetting-curve and interleaving percentages in one source were left out because they could not be verified.
- **Vendor connectors:** the k12 plugin's optional third-party education connectors and the Learning Commons Knowledge Graph are not used; the guides work without them.
