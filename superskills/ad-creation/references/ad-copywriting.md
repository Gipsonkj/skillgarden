# Ad copywriting: hooks, headlines, primary text, CTAs

Every ad has one job: get the right person to stop, read and take one action. One message, one CTA. Character limits for each platform are in [platform-specs.md](platform-specs.md).

## Rules for every line

1. **Specific beats vague.** "Cut weekly reporting from 4 hours to 15 minutes" beats "Save time".
2. **Outcome beats feature.** Feature: two-factor login. Advantage: bank-level security. Benefit: your customer data stays yours. Stop at the benefit, not the feature.
3. **Customer words beat company words.** Lift phrasing from reviews and comments verbatim.
4. **Numbers when you have them.** "3x faster", "in 5 minutes", "11,000 teams". Only real ones.
5. **Active voice, plain words.** "Use" not "utilize". Cut "very", "really", "almost".
6. **Clear beats clever.** If the reader has to decode it, they scroll.
7. **Swap test.** If the line would work unchanged for a competitor, rewrite it. If you lack the differentiator, write `[NEED: differentiator]`.
8. **"Now you can..." test.** Prefix the headline with "Now you can". If the result is not both compelling and true, rewrite.
9. **The ad can't promise what the page doesn't deliver.** See message match in [creative-strategy.md](creative-strategy.md).

## Headline formulas

| Formula | Shape | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Outcome + time | [outcome] in [time] | "Get your tax refund in 10 days" |
| Outcome without pain | [outcome] without [pain] | "Build your site without writing code" |
| Turn X into Y | Turn [input] into [outcome] | "Turn one-time buyers into regulars" |
| Problem question | [pain as their question] | "Still exporting to CSV every Monday?" |
| Stop / start | Stop [pain]. Start [gain]. | "Stop chasing invoices. Get paid on day 1." |
| For audience | [category] for [audience] | "Payroll for 5-person restaurants" |
| Proof | [number] [people] [outcome] | "3,400 clinics book with [X]" |
| Category + difference | The [category] that [differentiator] | "The CRM that updates itself" |
| How-to | How [persona] [outcome] | "How growth teams cut CAC 35% in a quarter" |
| Objection | answer the main blocker | "Works with the POS you already have" |
| Comparison | vs. the current way | "Cheaper than a bookkeeper. Faster than a spreadsheet." |
| Real urgency | [real deadline or limit] | "Q1 pricing ends 31 March" |

Use a formula as a starting shape, then fill every slot with a fact.

## Copy frameworks for body text

| Framework | Structure | Best for |
|---|---|---|
| PAS | Problem in their words → what it costs them (don't exaggerate) → product as the fix | Cold feed; works short or long |
| BAB | Before (today) → after (goal) → bridge (product) | Consideration, case studies, retargeting |
| FAB | Feature → advantage → benefit | Decision stage, product-page visitors |
| AIDA | Attention → interest → desire (proof) → action | LinkedIn, longer formats |
| Social proof lead | Open with a real stat or quote → what they got → CTA | Retargeting, decision |
| Contrarian | Name a belief the reader holds → the fact that corrects it | Cold, X, LinkedIn |
| How-it-works | 3 steps → outcome → CTA | Complex products |

Platform fit: Google RSA = specificity and benefit-first (they searched). Meta cold = PAS or contrarian. Meta retargeting = BAB or proof. LinkedIn = AIDA, FAB, how-it-works, insight-first. TikTok = compressed PAS, hook-first. X = contrarian or specific.

## Primary text structure (Meta, LinkedIn)

```
Line 1 (inside the visible 125/150): the hook. A specific pain, result or question.
Lines 2-4: 2-3 concrete points or one short story beat. Line breaks between them.
Last line: the CTA with what they get, plus the real offer if there is one.
```

Front-load. Everything after "See more" is only for people already interested. Use 0-2 emoji, only where they add meaning (none on LinkedIn or Google).

## Descriptions

Descriptions add what the headline didn't: a proof point, an objection answered ("No card required"), the offer, or a CTA. Never repeat the headline.

## CTAs

- Formula: **verb + what they get + qualifier**. "Start my 14-day trial", "Get the pricing for my team", "See it on your own data".
- Weak: Submit, Sign up, Learn more, Click here, Get started today, Join thousands of happy customers.
- The CTA must match the landing page's actual action.

## No AI tells (check the whole batch)

Generated copy repeats the same tics across 50 variations. Ban these:

| Pattern | Example to avoid | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Contrast reveal | "It's not X, it's Y." "Not because X. Because Y." | State Y with the reason |
| Negation list | "No setup. No fees. No contracts." | Say what happens. One real absence near the CTA is fine. |
| Trailing pile-on | "...syncs automatically, ensuring nothing slips." | End the sentence at the claim |
| Self-answered question | "The result? 3x faster." | Just state it |
| Colon reveal | "The best part: it learns." | Normal sentence |
| Stock phrases | "Say goodbye to", "X, reimagined", "Unlock the power of", "Take it to the next level", "Here's the thing", "In today's fast-paced world" | Replace with the fact |
| Puffery words | seamless, robust, powerful, innovative, streamline | Replace with the fact they stand for |
| Em dashes | in headlines, descriptions, primary text | Comma, full stop, or rewrite |
| Hook slop | generic "POV:", "Stop scrolling", "Nobody tells you this about X" | A hyper-specific situation ("POV: it's 3pm and you're on your fourth coffee") is fine |
| CTA slop | "Get started today" | Name the action and the gain |

Caps: at most one fragment and one list of three per ad. Vary sentence openings across a batch; ten variations that all start the same way read as generated.

## Policy and rejection triggers

| Trigger | Fix |
|---|---|
| ALL CAPS words (over 3 letters, not acronyms) | Sentence or title case |
| Repeated punctuation (!!!, ???, ...) | One mark max |
| Unproven superlatives ("#1", "best", "top-rated", "Best X of 2026") | Remove, or cite the source on the page |
| "Guaranteed" results, income claims ("Make $10k/month") | Show real data or drop it |
| Platform names in copy ("Facebook", "Instagram") on Meta | Remove |
| Competitor trademarks | Remove or get permission; comparisons must be provable |
| Personal attributes ("Are you depressed?", "Single and over 50?") | Rephrase without implying you know the viewer's traits |
| Click-bait ("You won't believe...", "Click here") | Specific promise and CTA |
| Fake urgency (countdowns with no real deadline) | Real date or remove |
| Health before/after, finance, earnings | Check the platform's category policy; flag for review |

Some templates in the wild use "#1", "As seen in" and "Best of [year]" headlines. Don't: they need substantiation, and press-logo ads carry rights risk.

## Producing a set

1. Pick 3-5 angles ([creative-strategy.md](creative-strategy.md)).
2. Per angle, write variations that change word choice, specificity (number vs. none), tone (statement, question, command) and length.
3. Generate in waves: core angles (3-5 × 5 variations) → extend the strongest 2 angles → 1-2 wild cards (contrarian, emotional, hyper-specific).
4. Filter: over-limit lines, near-duplicates, policy risks, headline/description pairs that don't make sense together.
5. Validate with the script (below), then present.

## Validate with the script

`scripts/ad-creative-alirezarezvani/ad_copy_validator.py` (Python 3, no dependencies) checks character limits, ALL CAPS, repeated punctuation, platform trademarks, risky phrases and unproven claims, and scores each ad 0-100.

```bash
python3 scripts/ad-creative-alirezarezvani/ad_copy_validator.py ads.json
echo '{"platform":"google_rsa","headlines":["..."],"descriptions":["..."]}' | python3 scripts/ad-creative-alirezarezvani/ad_copy_validator.py
```

Input is one object or a list. `platform` is `google_rsa`, `google_display`, `meta_feed` (`primary_text`, `headline`), `linkedin` (`intro_text`, `headline`), `twitter` or `tiktok` (`primary_text`). The Meta and LinkedIn text checks use the visible-preview limits (125, 150), so a long primary text is flagged as a truncation warning, not a hard error. Fix every flag or explain why it stays.
